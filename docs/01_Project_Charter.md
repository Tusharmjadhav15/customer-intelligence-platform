# 1. Project Overview

The Customer Intelligence Platform (CIP) is an end-to-end machine learning application designed to help e-commerce businesses understand customer behaviour, improve customer retention, and support data-driven business decisions.

The platform will integrate data ingestion, analytics, machine learning models, REST APIs, and an interactive dashboard into a single application.

---

# 2. Business Problem

Many small and medium-sized e-commerce businesses collect customer and sales data but struggle to transform it into actionable insights.

Business teams often rely on manual reports and basic dashboards, making it difficult to:

- Identify valuable customers
- Detect customers likely to churn
- Estimate customer lifetime value
- Recommend relevant products
- Support strategic marketing decisions

The absence of predictive analytics limits business growth and customer engagement.

---

# 3. Business Objectives

The project aims to:

- Improve customer understanding through analytics.
- Predict customer churn.
- Estimate customer lifetime value (CLV).
- Segment customers based on purchasing behaviour.
- Generate personalised product recommendations.
- Provide business insights through interactive dashboards.
- Reduce manual reporting efforts.
